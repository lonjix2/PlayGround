LIGHTBEARER

City of Refuge

“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.” — Psalm 119:105

🏘️ Free-roam town📖 Scripture combat🙏 Deliver NPCs🌑 Fight shadows🛡️ Resist temptation

Move with WASD / left stick. Look with mouse (click canvas to lock).

1–4 or J / ✝ — Speak the equipped Scripture. Each scroll burns Light ✨. Matching a shadow's weakness deals double damage.

E / 💬 — Talk to townsfolk. Choose the right Scripture for their affliction to deliver them.

Q / 🙏 (hold) — Pray to restore Light and heal. Stand in the church glow for faster renewal.

B / 📖 — Open your Bible: equip scrolls & re-read verses.

Resist temptation whispers by answering with the true Word before time runs out. Falling costs Peace 🕊; losing all Faith ✝ means you rest at the church.

Deliver souls, cleanse shadows, ring the church bell 🔔 to finish your witness.

⭐ Grace Points: right Scripture for the right need earns big points — Deliverance +120 · Perfect hit +25 · Cleanse +80 · Resist temptation +100 · Blessing +10 · Scroll +15. Consecutive correct answers build a 🔥 streak bonus (+5 per streak, up to +50). Wrong answers break the streak (no points lost — grace!).

🖥️ Desktop build: npm run dist (Electron — Windows / Linux / macOS) · also playable in browser

📖 Your Scrolls

Equip up to 4 scrolls to your hotbar (click a scroll, then click a slot — or press 1–4 while selected). Matching categories matters: Deliverance ✝ breaks Fear · Peace 🕊 calms Anger · Strength 💪 lifts Despair · Faith 🔥 answers Doubt.

🕊️ You rest at the church…

“He restoreth my soul.” — Psalm 23:3. Your Faith is renewed. Rise and shine again, lightbearer.

🔔 The bell rings over the city!
